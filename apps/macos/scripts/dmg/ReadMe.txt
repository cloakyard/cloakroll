CloakRoll — Beta Install Guide

INSTALL
  1. Drag CloakRoll onto Applications in this window.
  2. Open CloakRoll from Applications, then eject the installer.

  This beta is locally ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple.
  If macOS cannot verify it, only proceed if you trust its source and have
  checked the supplied checksum. After attempting to open it, use System
  Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, then confirm Open.
  Apple’s guidance: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

FIRST BACKUP
  Connect and unlock your iPhone over USB; accept Trust if asked. Choose
  a backup folder, then back up new items or a selection. Each iPhone
  remembers its folder. Folder organization is in Settings → Backup.
  Right-click the destination to open it in Finder.

  Only originals exposed by the phone over USB are available. iCloud-only
  items may be absent. CloakRoll does not convert or delete originals.
  Keep the Mac awake with its lid open and the phone connected until the
  backup finishes. Stop waits for the current operation to settle.

HISTORY AND RECOVERY
  Check saved files from Backup History. To rebuild lost app history,
  choose Rebuild History and your backup folder. Keep the hidden
  .cloakroll-recovery records when copying or moving that folder.
  Older media-only folders can be verified once with the original iPhone.

BETA LIMITS
  Keep another independent copy of irreplaceable originals while testing.
  Final cable/removable-drive interruption, full-disk, sustained performance,
  full assistive-technology and macOS 26 runtime checks remain open.
  Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later. Intel Macs are not
  supported. Current runtime validation is on Apple silicon with macOS 27. Restore to iPhone is not implemented.

PRIVACY AND FEEDBACK
